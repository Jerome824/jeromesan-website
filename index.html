Core Investigation · The Peak

Closing the Global Emission Gap

Bridging ambient thermodynamic realities with comprehensive multi-scale sustainability. Grounding Engineered Negative Emission Technologies (E-NETs) and Direct Air Capture (DAC) in time-varying regional dynamics, life cycle carbon honesty, and multi-criteria trade-offs.

01 / DYNAMIC DAC MODELING SA-TVSA CYCLE

Climate-Driven Direct Air Capture

Dynamic modeling of steam-assisted temperature vacuum swing adsorption (SA-TVSA) powered by heat pumps under time-varying climate data. Examining thermodynamic penalties, water-CO₂ co-adsorption, and techno-economics across four distinct climate zones (Xining, Chengdu, Lanzhou, Guiyang).

STAGE 01 Adsorption
Ambient CO₂ & H₂O co-uptake
STAGE 02 Desorption
SA-TVSA thermal swing ~100°C
STAGE 03 Efficacy
70.61% peak CCE in Xining
