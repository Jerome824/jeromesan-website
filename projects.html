Journal Article · 2026 环境工程学报 (Chinese Journal of Environmental Engineering)

工程负排放技术可持续性评价的方法与指标：研究现状与发展趋势

Methods and indicators for sustainability assessment of engineered negative emission technologies: Current status and future trends

DOI: 10.12030/j.cjee.202606022

Systematically synthesizes the life cycle assessment (LCA) frameworks and multi-criteria sustainability metrics for engineered negative emission technologies (NETs), establishing methodological guidelines for net-negative carbon verification.

Journal Article · 2026 洁净煤技术 (Clean Coal Technology)

时变气候数据驱动下直接空气碳捕集系统动态建模及技术经济性分析

Dynamic modeling and techno-economic analysis of direct air carbon capture systems driven by time-varying climate data

Develops a dynamic mathematical model of solid-sorbent Direct Air Capture (DAC) coupled with meteorological variations (humidity and ambient temperature), evaluating levelized cost of CO₂ removal under fluctuating environmental conditions.
