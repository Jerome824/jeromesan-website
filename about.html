Identity, Research & Craft

Understanding the mind behind the models: high sensitivity, thermodynamic rigor, technical tooling, and offline passions.

1. Introduction & Personality Profile

I go by Jerome Lau. I am fundamentally an introvert who identifies deeply with the Highly Sensitive Person (HSP) and INFJ personality archetypes. In a world characterized by velocity and noise, high sensitivity allows me to notice nuanced patterns, subtle shifts in systemic parameters, and the broader humanitarian consequences of engineered solutions.

Being an INFJ in engineering means approaching technical problems not just as numerical puzzles, but as systemic missions. I value deliberate quiet, deep focused blocks of work, and holistic conceptual integrity over superficial speed.

2. Research Domains & Engineering Track

I am currently a 3rd-year Power Engineering student. My investigative focus centers on Direct Air Capture (DAC) technologies and comprehensive Life Cycle Assessment (LCA).

Specifically, I research the dynamic behavior of solid-sorbent DAC systems under fluctuating meteorological boundaries, seeking ways to minimize the specific energy consumption of thermal desorption. Concurrently, I utilize LCA frameworks to calculate the genuine cradle-to-grave net negativity of negative-emission systems, auditing upstream electricity carbon intensity and embodied material footprints.
