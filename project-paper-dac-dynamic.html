时变气候数据驱动下直接空气碳捕集系统动态建模及技术经济性分析

Dynamic modeling and techno-economic analysis of direct air carbon capture systems driven by time-varying climate data

中文摘要 · Abstract 中图分类号: X701
直接空气碳捕集（DAC）技术具有选址灵活、可模块化部署等优势，已成为实现净零排放目标的关键负排放技术。其中，基于固体吸附法的DAC技术因其解吸温度低、易与低品位热源耦合等特点，具有大规模应用前景。然而，既有研究多局限于稳态运行假设，未能充分考虑实际运行中大气温度、湿度动态变化对DAC性能的显著影响，难以反应其在动态工况下的表现。

基于此，本研究构建了考虑气象参数波动的蒸汽辅助真空变温吸附DAC动态系统，并选取西宁、成都、兰州和贵阳等四个典型气象区进行案例分析。结果表明，DAC的能效水平、碳捕集效率以及经济性均依赖气候与能源清洁度的协同，即在干冷且采用低碳能源结构的区域，系统才能兼顾净碳移除与技术经济可行性。评估显示，在低温干燥地区，如西宁的气象条件有利于提升产率和热力学效率，达成最低1042.6 $/t CO₂的捕集成本及70.61%的碳捕集效率。但在湿度和电网碳强度较高的区域如兰州，由于高热能需求与碳排放增加，导致成本攀升至近2400 $/t CO₂而碳捕集效率降至负值，无法实现负排放。因此，DAC技术的规模化部署与选址应该基于实际工况进行评估，以确保其技术经济可行性与负排放目标的实现。
关键词：直接空气碳捕集；负排放技术；气象影响；动态模拟；技术经济评估
English Abstract SA-TVSA System
Direct air capture (DAC) technology, with advantages such as flexible siting and modular deployment, has emerged as a key negative emission technology for achieving net-zero emission targets. Among various approaches, solid sorbent-based DAC technology holds promise for large-scale application due to its low desorption temperature and ease of integration with low-grade heat sources. However, existing studies are mostly confined to steady-state operating assumptions and fail to adequately account for the significant impacts of dynamic variations in ambient temperature and humidity on DAC performance under real-world conditions, thus making it difficult to reflect system behavior under dynamic operating scenarios.

To address this gap, this study develops a dynamic model of a steam-assisted vacuum temperature swing adsorption (SA-TVSA) DAC system that incorporates fluctuating meteorological parameters, and conducts case studies across four typical climatic regions: Xining, Chengdu, Lanzhou, and Guiyang. The results indicate that the energy efficiency, carbon capture efficiency, and economic viability of DAC all depend on the synergy between climate conditions and energy cleanliness that means in regions with cold, dry climates and a low-carbon energy mix can the system simultaneously achieve net carbon removal and techno-economic feasibility. The assessment shows that in low-temperature, arid regions such as Xining, favorable meteorological conditions enhance productivity and thermodynamic efficiency, achieving a minimum capture cost of 1,042.6 USD/t CO₂ and a carbon capture efficiency of 70.61%. However, in regions with high humidity and high grid carbon intensity, such as Lanzhou, increased thermal energy demand and carbon emissions drive the cost up to nearly 2,400 USD/t CO₂, while carbon capture efficiency drops to negative values, rendering net negative emissions unattainable. Therefore, the large-scale deployment and siting of DAC technology should be evaluated based on actual operating conditions to ensure both techno-economic feasibility and the realization of negative emission goals.
Keywords: Direct air capture; negative emission technologies; meteorological impacts; dynamic simulation; techno-economic evaluation

SA-TVSA 循环五阶段动态吸附与解吸过程

STAGE 01
吸附 (Adsorption)
风机驱动环境空气流经接触器，使空气中 trace CO₂ 与水蒸气在胺基位点发生温湿度依赖的共吸附。
STAGE 02
抽真空 (Evacuation)
腔室完全密封，真空泵强力抽取排出床层内部间隙空气，降低系统残余压力至真空基准。
STAGE 03
加热解吸 (Heating)
电网驱动的热泵输入低品位清洁热能，在真空环境下加热吸附剂，大量释放吸附的 CO₂ 与 H₂O。
STAGE 04
蒸汽吹扫 (Steam Purge)
通入低压饱和蒸汽降低接触器内部 CO₂ 分压，显著拓宽树脂的工作容量并加速解吸脱附效率。
STAGE 05
冷却再生 (Cooling)
冷却塔流体循环快速带走吸附床余热，使吸附树脂恢复初始冷态，就绪启动下一个完整工作循环。
