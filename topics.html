Thermodynamics · 422 ppm Ambient CO₂
Topic 01 · Planetary Engineering

Direct Air Capture (DAC) Systems

Directly extracting CO₂ from ambient air via chemical sorbents and thermal-fluid cycles. Focuses on reducing parasitic regeneration power and optimizing contactor fluid dynamics under real-world weather conditions.

  • Solid Amine Sorbents: Chemical sorption loops with low-temperature desorption (~100°C).
  • Air Contactor Aerodynamics: Minimizing static pressure drops and fan electricity demand.
  • Dynamic Modeling: Analyzing how temperature and relative humidity alter capture capacity.

ISO 14040/44 · Net-Negativity Audits
Topic 02 · Environmental Accountability

Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) Frameworks

Quantifying full supply-chain footprints for negative emission technologies (NETs). Ensuring that energy inputs and material synthesis do not negate atmospheric carbon removal benefits.

  • Cradle-to-Grave Boundaries: Embodied carbon in steel, concrete, and sorbent manufacturing.
  • Upstream Grid Intensity: Sourcing clean power vs displacing fossil generation.
  • Sustainability Indicators: Water usage, chemical degradation, and mineral permanence metrics.

Topic 03 · Applied Modeling

Techno-Economic & Climate Dynamics

Coupling meteorological time-series datasets to capital cost evaluations (CAPEX/OPEX). Assessing operational flexibility when DAC facilities operate alongside intermittent solar and wind resources.

  • Time-Varying Climate Data: Hourly ambient temperature and humidity impact modeling.
  • Levelized Cost Calculation: Computing cost per ton CO₂ across 25-year plant lifetimes.
  • Grid Integration: Flexible scheduling to avoid peak electricity pricing tariffs.
